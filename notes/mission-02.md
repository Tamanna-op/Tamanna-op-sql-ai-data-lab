# Mission 2 – Filtering Data

## 🎯 Objective

Learn how to filter rows using the WHERE clause and combine conditions with AND and OR.

---

## 🧠 Key Learnings

### WHERE
Filters rows that match a condition.

Example:
```sql
SELECT * FROM researchers
WHERE team = 'Vision';
```

### AND
Returns rows only when **all** conditions are true.

### OR
Returns rows when **at least one** condition is true.

---

## 💡 Professional Tips

- Always read the business requirement before writing SQL.
- Use parentheses when combining AND and OR to avoid logical mistakes.
- Start with simple conditions, then combine them.
